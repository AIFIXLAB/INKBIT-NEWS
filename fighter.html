District 8 · The Arena

Inkbit Fighter

5,555 fighters. 20 styles. One Grand Champion.

WHERE THEY CAME FROM

Every year, when the ink runs hot, the Kingdom clears the old quarry between the Capital and the Pet Meadows, rings it with lanterns and calls it the Arena. Then it holds the Inkbit Tournament.

Fighters come from every corner of the map: karate masters from the Capital, Muay Thai fighters from the harbour, shadow ninjas from the rooftops of Ink Street, cyborgs rebuilt in the Danger Zone, and an oni or two that nobody invited.

WHO THEY ARE

5,555 fighters entered this year, in twenty styles: Karate, Muay Thai, Samurai, Sumo, Boxer, Luchador, Shadow Ninja, Kabuki, Oni, Cyborg, Inferno, Frost, Thunder and more.

The bracket is merciless. One Grand Champion, two Finalists, four Semi-Finalists, eight Quarter-Finalists, then rounds of 16, 32, 64 and onwards to 1,024. Everyone else is a Challenger, still hungry for a shot.

WHAT THEY DO

They fight for the title, and for the crowd. Nobody knows who reached the final until the reveal. Round 1. Fight!

The Inkbit Tournament opens tonight

Oct 29, 2026

The lanterns around the Arena are lit. At 20:00 tonight the Inkbit Tournament opens its gates to 5,555 fighters in twenty styles, from karate masters and Muay Thai fighters to luchadors, cyborgs and a few oni.

The first 2,000 fighters get in free. After that the entry fee rises in three steps, roughly $0.03, $0.06 and $0.09. Three fighters per wallet, ten days to enter, and nobody learns who reached the final until the reveal.
