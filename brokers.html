WHERE THEY CAME FROM

As the Kingdom grew, everything it printed started to get a price. Next to the castle, a street that never sleeps grew up around one building: the Inkbit Exchange, on Ink Street.

Inside, giant screens draw candles all day. Green ink when things go up, red ink when they go down.

WHO THEY ARE

The Inkbit Brokers hold the pens. Every one of them is somewhere on the ladder, from Intern to Founder, and you can tell how high they've climbed by what they wear: a T-shirt and a visitor badge at the bottom, a tuxedo, a gold tie and a crown at the top.

On Ink Street, people wear the market on their heads: Bull horns when it goes up, Bear ears when it goes down, a Pig suit for anyone who sold too early, a Whale suit and a spoon for scooping the dip, a Shark fin, and Moth antennae for the newcomers.

WHAT THEY DO

They trade everything the Kingdom has ever printed, on ten desks: Ape, Card, Garage, World, Pet, Zombie, Tribe, Gold, Ink and Night. Lately the Tribe Desk has been very busy with one rumour: Pure Ink.

Every hut on the Uncharted Isle is taken

Oct 29, 2026

All 4,444 Wild Ones have been claimed, a day before the boats were due to stop running. They went to 1,472 different households across the Kingdom, and the trading posts on Ink Street have been busy ever since.

Brokers on the Tribe Desk report a steady queue of buyers since the gates closed: well over a hundred Wild Ones changed hands in the first day, and the cheapest one on offer has nearly doubled in price since yesterday. Nobody knows yet who holds the Supreme Chief or the ten Tribe Chiefs. That stays sealed until the reveal.
