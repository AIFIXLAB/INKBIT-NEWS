Inkbit Apes

1,111 pixel apes printed in ink. 11 came out wrong.

WHERE THEY CAME FROM

Apepixel City, New Year's Eve, 1999. A power surge hit a tiny back-alley print shop and woke up an old dot-matrix printer that had spent thirty years printing menus and lottery slips.

It didn't print menus. It printed apes, pixel by pixel, in thick black ink. By the time the fuse blew there were 1,111 sheets on the floor.

WHO THEY ARE

The Inkbit Apes are the founders of the Kingdom. Every other district, every other character, traces back to the ink that came out of that printer.

Eleven sheets came out wrong: inverted, double-struck, melted. The shop owner called them the Misprints and slipped them back into the stack. Nobody knows which numbers they are hiding behind.

WHAT THEY DO

The apes still run Apepixel City. They keep the old press alive, and the press still prints: Press Cards for the shop's workers, and sealed crates down at the Harbour Garage for the apes who hold enough of both.
