Inkbit Press Cards

555 free cards from the Inkbit print shop. 10 designs. One Misprint.

WHERE THEY CAME FROM

The apes kept the old press running. One night it started printing portraits of the people who worked the shop: the Paper Boy, the Ink Runner, the Typesetter, the Ink Chemist, the Head Printer.

And one card nobody could explain: The Misprint.

WHO THEY ARE

Press Cards are the Kingdom's tickets. They minted out in about twenty minutes, to 191 holders.

WHAT THEY DO

Holding cards is how you qualify for rewards. One card and two Apes put you in the draw for a free Ape. Three cards and five Apes earn a sealed crate from the Harbour Garage: 20 motorbikes, 15 cars, 10 houses and 5 legendary pieces.
