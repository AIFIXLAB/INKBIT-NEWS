District 3 · The Capital

Inkbit World

2,468 citizens of the Capital. 100 jobs. 79 seats in the government.

WHERE THEY CAME FROM

From the print shop the ink spread outward. Streets were drawn, then squares, then a castle. The Capital grew into the heart of the Kingdom, and 2,468 citizens moved in.

WHO THEY ARE

Nobody in the Capital wears a uniform. You can tell what a citizen does by what is on their head, on their face and behind their ear: a chef's toque, a pilot's cap, lab goggles, a stethoscope, a pencil. One hundred professions keep the city running, about twenty-four citizens each.

Seventy-nine citizens run the government: one Governor, three Ministers (Ink, Trade and Peace), a City Lord for each of the five cities, ten Council members, twenty Officers and forty City Staff. Nobody knows who holds which seat until the reveal.

WHAT THEY DO

They run the Kingdom from the castle. Every road on the map leads back to the Capital, including Ink Street, where the Inkbit Exchange stands.
