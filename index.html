THE STORY SO FAR

As reported by Inkbit News
CHAPTER 01
The night the printer woke up

Apepixel City, New Year's Eve, 1999. A power surge hit a tiny back-alley print shop and woke its old dot-matrix printer, and it began printing apes, pixel by pixel, in thick black ink. By the time the fuse blew, 1,111 sheets lay on the floor. Eleven came out wrong: the Misprints.

CHAPTER 02
The press keeps running

The apes kept the old press alive. It printed cards of the people who worked the shop, the Paper Boy, the Ink Chemist, the Head Printer, and one card nobody could explain: The Misprint. The cards became the Kingdom's tickets to rewards.

CHAPTER 03
A kingdom drawn in ink

From the shop, the ink spread outward. Streets and squares were drawn, and 2,468 citizens moved into the Capital, Inkbit World. The Kingdom had one rule: whatever is drawn here, lives here.

CHAPTER 04
The pets come home

Every home needed a guardian. Cats, dogs, bears, tigers and rabbits were drawn into the Pet District, and the wildest among them, a Golden Dragon, three Royals and nine Wild Legends, refused to stay in their baskets.

CHAPTER 05
The last bottle of bad ink

Deep in the print shop, one bottle had gone bad since that night in 1999. Wrong colour, wrong smell. When it finally spilled, whoever was drawn with it turned green and grey, and their lines began to rot. The city called it Inkrot.

CHAPTER 06
The quarantine breaks

The infected were sealed behind hazard tape at the edge of the map, in a district renamed the Danger Zone. On October 30 the tape gives way. Who walks out, and at what stage of infection, will only be known after the reveal.

CHAPTER 07
The island at the edge of the map

Far east, past the Danger Zone, lies an island no map-maker ever finished. Long before 1999, its people ground charcoal, red clay, berries and bone into the First Ink. Ten tribes, each keeping one colour. They call themselves the Wild Ones.

CHAPTER 08
Is anyone mixing a cure?

The Ink Chemist has gone missing. Some say she sailed east to find Pure Ink, the recipe only the First Flame knows and the one thing that could cure Inkrot. The Wild Ones have never let an outsider onto their island. Until now. To be continued.
