THE STORY SO FAR

As reported by Inkbit News
CHAPTER 01
A kingdom drawn in ink

Inkbit World was drawn one pixel at a time, by hand, in ink. Citizens filled the squares, and every face was different. The Kingdom had one rule: whatever is drawn here, lives here.

CHAPTER 02
The pets come home

Every home needed a guardian. Cats, dogs, bears, tigers and rabbits were drawn into the Pet District, and the wildest among them, a Golden Dragon, three Royals and nine Wild Legends, refused to stay in their baskets.

CHAPTER 03
Bad ink in the old print shop

A bottle spilled in a print shop at the edge of the map. Wrong colour, wrong smell. Whoever was drawn with it turned green and grey, and their lines began to rot. The first three, Patient Zero, still glow in the dark.

CHAPTER 04
The quarantine breaks

The district was sealed behind hazard tape and renamed the Danger Zone. On October 30 the tape gives way. Who walks out, and at what stage of infection, will only be known after the reveal.

CHAPTER 05
Is anyone drawing a cure?

Rumours say an inkmaker in the Capital is mixing something new. Holders across the Kingdom will be the first to read it here. To be continued.
